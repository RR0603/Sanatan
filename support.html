Support

Pranam 🙏 Here are answers to the most common questions. Still stuck? Email support@livingsanatan.com with your phone model and what you see.

The sky doesn't follow my phone

Tap the round compass button at the bottom (or “Follow my phone”) and allow Motion & Orientation. If you once tapped “Don't Allow”, close the app completely and open it again. On iPhone you can also check Settings → Antariksha (or Settings → Safari for the web version).

North looks a few degrees off

Move your phone in a figure-of-eight to calibrate the compass, and keep away from metal, magnets and magnetic phone cases. Tilting the phone forward for a moment when you start helps it lock north. You can fine-tune north by dragging sideways while the sensor is on, or in Settings → Compass correction.

The camera view is black

Allow camera access when asked. If you denied it, enable it in your phone's Settings for Antariksha.

Restore my Pro purchase

Open the menu (☰) → Antariksha Pro → Restore purchase, while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

My birth chart differs from another app

Antariksha uses the sidereal zodiac with Lahiri (Chitrapaksha) ayanamsa, whole-sign houses and the mean lunar node — the most common choices in India. Other apps may use a different ayanamsa, house system or the true node. Also check the birth time zone and place.

Is my data uploaded?

No. Your location, camera, sensors and birth details stay on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
