Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026

Antariksha (“the app”) is made by Living Sanatan. We built it so that your sky, your place and your birth details stay with you. This policy explains exactly what the app does with information.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your location, camera, motion sensors and birth details are used only on your device and are never sent to us.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. Our purchase provider, RevenueCat, receives an anonymous identifier and your purchase record so the app knows Pro is unlocked.

Location

With your permission the app reads your approximate location to calculate the sky, sunrise, sunset and Panchang for where you are. The calculation happens on your device. Your last place is saved on the device so the app opens correctly next time. It is not uploaded. You can instead pick a city or type coordinates.

Camera

In camera (AR) view the live picture from your rear camera is shown behind the sky. Frames are displayed only; they are never recorded, saved or transmitted.

Motion and orientation sensors

Gyroscope, accelerometer and compass readings are used on the device to point the map where your phone points. They are not stored or sent anywhere.

Birth details and saved profiles

Names, birth dates, times and places you enter to cast a Janma Kundali or to match two charts (Kundali Milan) are stored only in the app's storage on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting a profile, clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes them.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders for the next few weeks are worked out and scheduled on your device, for the place you have chosen. No server is involved, and nothing about your reminders leaves your device. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in your phone's settings.

Purchases

Antariksha Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies. To confirm and restore your purchase we use RevenueCat, Inc., which receives an anonymous app user ID, your device platform and the store's purchase receipt. It does not receive your name, email, location or birth details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Website

The web version is served by our hosting provider, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security. The web version uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. Settings are kept in your browser's local storage.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update this page and its effective date. Questions: support@livingsanatan.com.
