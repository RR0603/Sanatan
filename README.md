# The Ember Crown

A side-scrolling kingdom-builder in the spirit of *Kingdom Two Crowns*, set on the river ghats of an ancient Indian kingdom. It is a single HTML file with no build step: open `index.html` in a browser to play.

## The story

The Akhand Jyoti, a flame that had burned for a thousand years, has gone out, and the night no longer ends. You wake on a riverbank wearing a warm crown, with no memory and a talking firefly named Jugnu for company. You follow the river down four ghats to the place where the Flame died, and on the way you find out who you are and what you traded away. There are three endings. The third unlocks only if you find all four memory shrines.

## How to play

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| ← → or A / D | Ride |
| Shift | Gallop (uses stamina) |
| ↓ or S | Hold at a marker to pay; anywhere else, drop a coin |
| Space / Enter | Continue the story |
| Esc / P, M | Pause, sound |

On phones, on-screen pads appear.

- **Recruit** wanderers at the reed camps by dropping a coin near them.
- **Arm** them at the racks by the fire: bows make archers (they hunt by day and guard by night), hammers make builders, scythes make farmers, and shields make knights.
- **Build** walls, raise the fire altar to unlock stronger walls, light diya posts to slow and burn the Hollow, and clear fields.
- **Survive** the nights. The Hollow want gold. If one touches you when your purse is empty, it takes the crown.
- **Advance** by mending each ghat's ferry. Up to 8 of your people and 30 coins come with you.

## What's different from Kingdom

- **Diya posts.** Lit lamps slow the Hollow and make them smoulder.
- **Memory shrines.** Offerings at a shrine bring back a piece of your past and grant a lasting blessing (Agni, Vayu, Prithvi, Soma).
- **Rahu's eclipse.** On blood-red nights the waves come twice as large.
- **War elephant.** It is slower than the horse but tramples small Hollow.
- **War conch.** In the final chapter, sound it at daybreak to march your army on the Rift and face the Hollow Crown.
- **Dawn offerings.** Each morning the town leaves gold at the fire.

Progress saves in your browser after every chapter.
